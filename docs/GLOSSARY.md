# Glossary: Indian civil litigation for engineers

Mental model: **a case is a ticket that stays open for five years.** Each hearing is a two-minute standup where the
ticket usually just gets a new due date ("tareekh pe tareekh"). Occasionally something actually happens.

## People

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Plaintiff / Defendant | who sued / who is being sued (lower court) |
| Petitioner / Respondent | same roles in the High Court (writs, revisions) |
| Appellant | the side that lost and appealed |
| Counsel / Advocate | lawyer. "Counsel for plaintiff" = plaintiff's lawyer |
| Junior | young lawyer working under a senior (Sai Kiran, in our data) |
| Senior Counsel | very senior lawyer, often sends juniors instead |
| Standing Counsel | a government body's regular lawyer (GHMC's lawyer in C23) |
| garu / amma | Telugu honorifics ("Gopal garu" ≈ "Mr. Gopal"; amma for an older woman) |

## Courts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| City Civil Court, Hyderabad | lower court where suits start. Courtrooms are numbered: IV Additional Chief Judge (IV ACJ), etc. |
| Junior Civil Judge | smaller-value cases (C35) |
| High Court for the State of Telangana | appeals and writs (cases against the government). Rooms are "Court Hall 7/12"; judges are "Justice X", written "X J" |

## Case numbers: `<type> No. <serial> of <year>`

| Prefix | Meaning |
|---|---|
| O.S. | Original Suit: a new case |
| I.A. | Interlocutory Application: a side request inside a case (e.g. "stop construction meanwhile"). A sub-task |
| A.S. | Appeal Suit: appeal against a final decision |
| W.P. | Writ Petition: High Court case against a government body |
| C.R.P. | Civil Revision Petition: ask the High Court to fix a lower court's procedural error |
| C.M.A. | Civil Miscellaneous Appeal: appeal against a side order (not the final judgment) |

## Stages of a suit (a pipeline)

1. **Plaint**: the complaint.
2. **Vakalat**: lawyer's signed authority to act. **Written Statement (WS)**: the defendant's reply.
3. **Framing of issues**: the judge writes the exact questions to decide. The test spec.
4. **Evidence**: witnesses. **PW1** = plaintiff's witness 1, **DW1** = defendant's witness 1.
   **Chief** = your own lawyer questions your witness (mostly by affidavit). **Cross** = the other side questions
   them; contradictions surface here. **Exhibits** Ex.A1 (plaintiff's doc 1), Ex.B3 (defendant's doc 3).
   **Deposition** = the written record of testimony.
5. **Arguments**, then **Judgment**.

Writ petitions are shorter: admission → counter-affidavit (government's reply) → reply affidavit / rejoinder → hearing.

## Hearing-day words

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cause list | the day's schedule per courtroom; **item no.** is the queue position |
| Listed for | what the hearing is meant to do |
| Not reached | the judge ran out of time; new date |
| Adjournment / seeking time | postponement / asking for one |
| Pass over | "call my case later today" |
| Memo | short formal note to the court ("counsel unwell") |
| Costs | a fine paid to the other side for wasting time |
| Last opportunity | final warning before the court refuses more time |
| Order sheet / docket | the court's official log of each hearing, 1–3 dry lines |
| Certified copy (CC) | stamped official copy of an order; must be applied for |
| Undertaking | a promise made to the court ("I will file X in 3 weeks") |
| Diary no. | receipt number for a filing |
| Interim order / stay / injunction | temporary freeze or "stop doing X" while the case runs |
| Synopsis | a short summary the judge asks for when the file is huge |
| Boycott | lawyers abstaining from work that day |
| Limitation | legal deadline to file; miss it and the right is lost |
